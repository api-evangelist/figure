# Figure

> Figure is a fintech lender and blockchain-native capital marketplace. Its Partner
> APIs let affiliates and originators run the HELOC (home equity line of credit)
> lifecycle — pre-qualification, inquiry, offer selection, documents — and let asset
> holders pull loan-tape and payment-history reports. APIs authenticate with an apikey
> header, front a Kong gateway, and support optional JWE/RSA payload encryption.

## APIs
- [HELOC Inquiries](https://docs.figure.com/heloc-inquiries/api): Manage the full HELOC inquiry lifecycle — start inquiry, add SSN/income/property, verify liens, select property and offer, fetch costs and documents (base https://api.figure.com).
- [HELOC Pre-qualification](https://docs.figure.com/heloc-pre-qualification/api): Non-licensed affiliate HELOC offer pre-qualification (v1 and v2).
- [Portfolio Manager](https://docs.figure.com/portfolio-manager/api): Query and download current and historical owned/pledged loan-tape and payment-history reports.
